# Governance
Nima Khaki is the human mission owner and maintainer. An AI implementation owner
assists under the public constitution. Same-author checks do not constitute an
independent human audit. Maintainer review governs contributions and releases;
frozen gates are mandatory. Changes to scope require a public ADR and explicit
human authorization for any expansion into clinical deployment. No such expansion
is authorized. Resolve disagreements with concrete counterexamples and evidence.
